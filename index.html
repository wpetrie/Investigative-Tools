43rd Annual Police Memorial

Halifax

Sign-up is open for the 43rd annual Police memorial Service hosted by Halifax Regional Police. Officers will leave CBRPS on Saturday 18 October and will be provided with a travel van to transport them to Halifax. They will be staying at the Cambridge Hotel in Halifax on Saturday night.

On Sunday 19 Oct 2024 at 12:00pm members will meet in the front lobby and head down to the Halifax Police headquarters, We will be forming up at 12:30 p.m. and stepping off at 12:45 p.m.  Where the parade will march to Gran Parade square, where the ceremony will take place from 1:00pm to 2:00pm. After the ceremony, members will return back to CBRM.
